Got questions?

Frequently asked questions

How do I use U-Downloader?

Copy a supported public media link and paste it into the downloader. On Android, you can also install U-Downloader from your browser menu, then use a post’s Share menu and choose U-Downloader. Available results depend on backend and platform support.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. The interface adapts to mobile phones, tablets and desktop screens. Actual downloading also depends on your browser and the returned media format.

Why is my link not working?

Check that the URL is correct and publicly accessible. Some links may be unsupported, unavailable or restricted by the original platform.

Is every post downloadable?

No. Availability depends on platform restrictions and backend support. Private or restricted content may not be accessible.

Can I use downloaded media anywhere?

Not necessarily. Respect copyright, creator permissions and the terms of the original platform. Only use media in ways you are authorized to use it.
